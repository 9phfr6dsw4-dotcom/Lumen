## Lumen 0.2.0

- **Lumen now updates itself.** It looks for a new version when it starts and every hour after that, then downloads and installs it and reopens by itself, once any brightness change has finished. Your settings and presets stay as they are.
- **Check for Updates…** is in the menu bar panel and in Lumen's app menu.
- **Settings → Updates** turns automatic checks and automatic installs on or off, and shows when Lumen last checked.
- Every update is signed, and Lumen refuses anything that isn't signed by its own key.
- Lumen now goes online only to check GitHub for a new version. It still has no accounts and no tracking.

Install this version by hand once (download `Lumen.zip`, replace the old app in Applications). After that, new versions arrive on their own.
